# YouTube

Part of [gtm](../gtm/SKILL.md): its copy sheet and boundaries apply here.

Google shows videos on the results page for some questions, and a demo that answers one keeps finding viewers long after it goes up. YouTube suits users who would rather talk than write. Videos come from [gtm-video](../gtm-video/SKILL.md); this skill covers what changes on YouTube.

## Demos for video results

1. Search the user's buyer questions and note which ones show video results. For software, search the "best X" query on a phone too; a row of videos there calls for a video.
2. Pick one specific product question.
3. Record the product completing that exact task, long-form first; cut short clips from it afterwards.
4. Name the task in the video title.
5. Add chapters starting at 00:00, and captions corrected by hand.
6. Answer related buyer questions in the description.
7. Embed the video next to the written answer on the user's site ([seo-content](../seo-content/SKILL.md)).

## Title and thumbnail first

Decide the title and thumbnail before making the video, because they decide whether anyone clicks. Study the title and thumbnail structures of outliers in the niche, videos that beat their creator's usual views, and reuse the structure with the user's own words and images. After publishing, watch retention per video to see where viewers leave.

## Feed it from other work

- Rebuild articles as demos and short clips that show the result at once ([gtm-organic](../gtm-organic/SKILL.md)).
- A recorded 5-minute call with a user can go out as a video before it becomes a thread and posts.
- Shorts follow the short-form method in [gtm-video](../gtm-video/SKILL.md#short-form).

## Research

Read the transcripts of the top videos on a buyer question to find what they leave unanswered ([seo-content](../seo-content/SKILL.md)).
