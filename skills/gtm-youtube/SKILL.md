# YouTube

Part of [gtm](../gtm/SKILL.md): its copy sheet and boundaries apply here.

Google shows video results for some questions, and AI answers cite YouTube often: in one vendor's data on 100K+ AI answers, it was the most-cited non-corporate source (Kumar 2026). A demo that answers a buyer question can surface in both. YouTube suits users who would rather talk than write. Videos come from [gtm-video](../gtm-video/SKILL.md); this skill covers what changes on YouTube.

## Demos for video results

1. Search the user's buyer questions and note which ones show video results. For software, search the "best X" query on a phone too; a row of videos there calls for a video.
2. Pick one specific product question.
3. Record the product completing that exact task, long-form first; cut short clips from it afterwards.
4. Name the task in the video title.
5. Add chapters in the description: the first at 00:00, at least three in ascending order, each at least 10 seconds, because Google prioritises the key moments set there. Add captions, reviewing automatic ones and fixing what was mistranscribed.
6. Answer related buyer questions in the description.
7. Embed the video next to the written answer on the user's site ([seo-content](../seo-content/SKILL.md)). The YouTube page carries the video result, because Google doesn't count a page where the video complements the text as a watch page.

## Title and thumbnail first

Decide the title and thumbnail before making the video, because a click is the first signal YouTube's recommender reads (Goodrow, YouTube). Study the title and thumbnail structures of outliers in the niche, videos that beat their creator's usual views, and reuse the structure with the user's own words and images.

After publishing:

- On a long-form video, with advanced features enabled on the channel, test up to 3 title and thumbnail pairs with YouTube Studio's A/B test on desktop; Shorts can't be tested. It picks the winner by watch time share, not clicks, because YouTube learned that a click doesn't mean the viewer actually watched.
- Read the audience retention report per video: "intro" is the share still watching at 30 seconds, where a low figure suggests the opening didn't match the title and thumbnail, and "typical retention" compares the video with the user's 10 latest of similar length.

## Feed it from other work

- Rebuild articles as demos and short clips that show the result at once ([gtm-organic](../gtm-organic/SKILL.md)).
- A recorded 5-minute call with a user can go out as a video before it becomes a thread and posts.
- Shorts follow the short-form method in [gtm-video](../gtm-video/SKILL.md#short-form).

## Research

Read the transcripts of the top videos on a buyer question to find what they leave unanswered ([seo-content](../seo-content/SKILL.md)).
