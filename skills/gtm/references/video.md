# Product video

A video looks expensive through restraint: every choice is deliberate, and nothing is there by accident.

1. **Rules.** Fix them per video type before production: about 3 colors, one background style, one typeface, one kind of music. Reuse them across videos so the brand reads as decided.
2. **Storyboard.** Design every frame before animating. One idea per shot, the key object centered, room to breathe, and a background that never competes with it.
3. **Motion.** Ease every keyframe; linear motion reads cheap. Move between scenes with transitions, cutting hard only when the message needs it, and vary the pace across the video.
4. **Music.** Pick tempo for the feeling: 60–80 BPM cinematic, 90–110 smooth, 115–123 sharp and premium, faster reads as hype.
5. **Sound.** After the edit, listen through and cut every effect that is too loud, out of place, or does not help the viewer understand the product.

Show the storyboard to the user before animating, because frames are cheap to change and animation is not. Claims on screen come from the copy sheet.
