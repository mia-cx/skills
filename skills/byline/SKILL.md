Read all four skills into context, in one turn, before you write:

- [talk-normal](../talk-normal/SKILL.md): prose without AI tells.
- [talk-like-mia](../talk-like-mia/SKILL.md): Mia's voice for anything posted or sent as her.
- [gtm](../gtm/SKILL.md): who the copy is for and the claims it may make.
- [seo](../seo/SKILL.md): the queries the page answers and the metadata it ships.

Then apply each one to the parts of the task it covers.
